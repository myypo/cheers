# Bolder

Raise a safe or flat part of a Cheers UI to the conviction the rest of the surface already implies, without rebuilding anything the request did not name. The reflex answer, more effects, is the opposite of bold; reject it first.

Know the target section and what must stay untouched. Ask once if neither the request nor the code makes it clear.

## Which "bolder"

An open direction round owns the word first. "Bolder" said while a direction decision in [new-work.md](new-work.md) is on the table is the re-roll register (foreign forms only, at full commitment), not this command. This command refines a surface whose world already shipped. A request for a new world rather than a stronger section is a redesign and routes through new-work.md.

## Visitor mode

- **Persuade + Experience:** bolder means a stronger point of view. Scale, type, imagery, color, pacing, and composition can push harder inside the committed world.
- **Operate + Read:** bolder rarely means theatrics; they undermine trust. It means stronger hierarchy, clearer weight contrast, one sharper accent, more committed density, a more legible signature move. The amplification is in clarity, not drama.

## Scope is sovereign

"Everything else stays" is literal. Touch only the named target. Do not restyle its neighbors, migrate the page to a new idea, or add colors, fonts, radii, shadows, or system primitives the surface does not already own. If the system genuinely cannot express the direction, ask before expanding it, naming the exact addition and the job it would do.

## Why it reads flat

A section usually reads flat for reasons its neighbors already solved. Look at what the rest of the page does that the target does not: display type at full strength, structural devices that carry meaning, the signature motif, density and pacing. A flat section usually opts out of the system's own strongest moves; it often has no clear focal point and a weak primary action. Bring it up to the expressive level its neighbors already reach, in the system's own vocabulary.

## State the system

Before editing, name:

- the target and its boundary;
- the one decisive move;
- which owned devices (type scale, motif, palette roles, imagery) it reuses at full strength;
- what around it gets quieter so the move reads;
- the interaction contract that must survive unchanged.

## Apply

Read [craft-floor.md](craft-floor.md) first.

- **Amplify what the system already owns.** Reuse its motif and type scale at full strength, turned up for this section rather than invented for it. The bolder version looks more like the same brand, not less.
- **Keep content true.** Existing claims are in scope: preserve them unless the user supplies replacements. If real evidence is essential and absent, ask for it.
- **Commit, then clarify.** Half-measures read as noise. Make the one decisive move completely, then quiet everything around it so the move is legible. If every element got louder, the section got flatter.
- **Give it its own rhythm.** The target reads as a peak in the scroll, a shift in density or pace from what surrounds it, not more of the same.
- **Color:** on Operate and Read surfaces accent carries meaning (primary action, selection, focus, status). On Persuade and Experience surfaces take the world's committed strategy to full strength; do not hedge.
- **Motion:** at most one purposeful moment, per [animate.md](animate.md).

## The skeleton test

Strip the copy out of the planned section and study the bare structure. Does it still say what this section is and why it matters through hierarchy and the system's devices alone? If it only works once the words return, the boldness is in the text size, not the design. An image or artifact slot names a job, an anchor and a piece of evidence, not a cue for a decorative photo; fill it with what the subject actually has.

## Cheers fit

- Boldness lands on rendered, backend-confirmed states and honest pending states. Never make success louder by showing it before the backend confirms.
- Do not add signals or client state to make the UI feel lively.
- Forms, actions, and update regions the section carried keep working the same way.
- Use `cheers` for implementation; format changed templates with `cargo cheers fmt --rustfmt <files>`.

## Verify

Inspect desktop and mobile in one batched round, fix everything in one batch, confirm with at most one more round. Answer each item with rendered or source evidence, not a bare yes:

- Everything outside the target is unchanged.
- No new color, font, or system primitive appeared unasked.
- The skeleton still says what the section is.
- The section is unmistakably the same brand, only more sure of itself.
- Contrast, keyboard paths, reduced motion, and state trust still hold.

When the target holds its own without pulling the page apart, hand off to `cheers-design polish`.
